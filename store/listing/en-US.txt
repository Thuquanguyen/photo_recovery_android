FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Photo Recovery helps you find photos and videos already on your device, save copies to a Restored album, free up storage, and keep selected media in a private vault. Scanning and cleaning run on your phone.

RESTORE COPIES
Browse photos and videos in your gallery, select what you want to keep, and export them into the Restored album (Pictures/Restored). Original quality stays on this device.

QUICK SWIPE CLEAN
Review items one by one. Swipe left to delete — Android shows its own confirmation — swipe right to move them into the app Vault, or skip to the next item. Videos play in the app.

DUPLICATE CLEANER
Scan your library with full-file hashing, group identical photos, keep one original, and delete extras to reclaim space.

SCREENSHOT CLEANER
Find screenshot albums and remove captures you no longer need.

SECURE VAULT
Move selected photos and videos into a private in-app folder on this device. Restore them to your gallery anytime.

PRIVACY
Photo Recovery does not upload your photos or videos to our servers. Scanning, cleaning, and vault storage happen on-device.

LANGUAGES
English, Tiếng Việt, Español, 日本語, Français, Deutsch, 한국어, 中文, Português.

PLEASE NOTE
This app works with photos and videos still available in your device media library. It cannot rebuild files that were permanently erased, lost after a factory reset, or stored only on a damaged card. Items you delete follow your phone’s own Recycle bin / Recently Deleted rules.
