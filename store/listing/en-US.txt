Photo Recovery: Clean Photos

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Organize your gallery: save copies, clean duplicates and screenshots, use Vault.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Photo Recovery is a simple tool for reviewing, organizing, and cleaning the photos and videos in your gallery. Save copies of important memories, find duplicates, clear unwanted screenshots, and move private media into Vault.

All gallery scanning and analysis happen on your device.

SAVE COPIES TO THE RESTORED ALBUM
Select photos or videos from your gallery and export copies to the Restored album (Pictures/Restored or Movies/Restored). Keep an additional copy on your device for easier organization.

QUICK SWIPE CLEAN
Review items one by one with simple gestures. Swipe left to delete with Android's system confirmation, swipe right to move an item into Vault, or skip to the next item. View photos and play videos directly in the app.

DUPLICATE CLEANER
Scan your library, group identical files, and select extra copies to remove. Keep the version you want while freeing storage space with confidence.

SCREENSHOT CLEANER
Find screenshots in your gallery and select several items to remove at once. It is a fast way to clean up captures you no longer need.

PRIVATE VAULT
Move selected photos and videos into a private space in the app. Restore them to your gallery whenever you need them.

PRIVACY
Your photos and videos are not uploaded to our servers by the app. Scanning, cleaning, and Vault are processed locally on your device.

LANGUAGES
English, Tiếng Việt, Español, 日本語, Français, Deutsch, 한국어, 中文, Português.

PLEASE NOTE
This app works with photos and videos that are still available in your device media library. It cannot recover files that were permanently deleted, lost after a factory reset, or stored only on a damaged memory card. Deleted items follow your device's Recycle Bin or Recently Deleted rules.
