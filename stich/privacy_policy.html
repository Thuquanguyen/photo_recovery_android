Privacy Policy — Photo Recovery

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Photo Recovery ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. The App is developed and maintained by the Photo Recovery team.
Contact: ngotuan101910@gmail.com

1. Data the App accesses on your device

To scan, preview, restore and clean up photos and videos, the App reads your device's media library (photos, videos and their metadata such as file name, size and date) via the Android MediaStore / Photos & Videos permission. This data is read and processed entirely on your device — the App does not upload your photos, videos, or their metadata to our servers, because we do not operate any backend server.

Files you choose to move to the in-app Vault, or restore from Recently Deleted, stay on your device's local storage; they are never transmitted off-device by the App.

2. Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob (banner, native, interstitial, app-open and rewarded ad formats) to keep the App free to use. AdMob may collect the advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / AD_ID permission), approximate device and network information, and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted in your region and consent settings. This data is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy.

You can reset or limit ad tracking at any time from your device's Settings > Google > Ads, or opt out of personalized ads where available in that same menu.

3. Permissions the App requests

  • Photos & Videos / Storage — to find, preview, recover and delete media you select.
  • Internet & Network State — required by the ad SDK to load ads and by the App to check remote ad configuration.
  • Notifications (Android 13+) — optional, used only if you enable scan-result alerts.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit your personal files to us.

4. Data we do not collect

We do not require account creation or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or the content of your photos/videos. We do not sell your data.

5. Data retention

Because the App has no backend, we do not retain copies of your media. Any local preference (such as selected language or Vault contents) stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its app data.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the App, contact us using the email above and we will address it.

7. Your choices

You can revoke the App's media/storage permission at any time from Android Settings > Apps > Photo Recovery > Permissions. Revoking it will prevent the App's core scan and recovery features from working.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where required, an in-app notice.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to ngotuan101910@gmail.com.
